# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Russian speakers learning Turkish vocabulary on a phone, in short daily sessions (about 3–5 minutes). They open the PWA between other tasks and expect to start swiping immediately — not to configure a study system.

## Product Purpose

LangApp teaches Turkish (TR→RU) through Tinder-style card swipes on a 3,564-word dictionary. Success is a completed 18-card session, a preserved streak, and coming back the next day. The product exists to make spaced repetition feel like a gesture, not homework.

## Brand Personality

Warm, tactile, confident. Voice is direct and unhurried: swipe, don't grind. The card is the hero; chrome stays quiet. Delight is reserved for named moments (session start, achievements, splash, account-setup wait), not sprinkled on every screen.

## Anti-references

- Cartoon-mascot language apps (Duolingo-style overload, endless XP chrome)
- Generic indigo SaaS dashboards and dark-mode developer tools
- Infinite feeds and “keep going” session lengths
- Centered CSS spinners as the only loading language
- Decorative bounce/elastic motion

## Design Principles

- Gesture first — the card is the product; everything else supports the swipe.
- Short sessions, no infinite scroll — 18 cards, then a clear stop.
- Recall before reveal — decide, then check.
- Familiar product UI, scarce delight — save choreography for openings and celebrations.
- Instant first paint — brand marks must not wait on the network.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast on UI text. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` with fade-only or static alternatives (no bounce). Touch targets stay large enough for one-handed phone use. Loading states must remain announced to assistive tech even when the visual splash is decorative.
